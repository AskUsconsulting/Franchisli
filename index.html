FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before booking a demo.

Is Franchisli right for my franchise size?

Franchisli is built for franchisors managing 20–500 locations. Whether you run a regional QSR network or a national hotel brand, the platform scales with you.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on the number of locations and features you need. No per-user charges — one flat rate per location tier. Custom quote available instantly after your demo.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. After your demo, you get 14 days of free access across up to 5 locations with all MVP features. No credit card required.

Do you have mobile apps?

Yes. iOS and Android apps are available for frontline staff. HQ and managers primarily use the web app, while staff handle daily tasks on mobile.

What is on the V1 roadmap?

V1 brings the full LMS, AI anomaly detection, corrective actions automation, real-time KPI dashboards, location benchmarking, and calendar integrations.

Can I migrate from FranConnect or Naranga?

Yes. We provide a white-glove migration service for SOPs, location data, and employee records. Most franchisors are fully migrated within two weeks.
